# MTT cytotoxicity protocol (lab SOP v3)

1. Seed HeLa cells at 5,000 cells per well in 96-well plates (100 µL DMEM + 10% FBS).
2. Incubate 24 h at 37 °C, 5% CO2.
3. Treat with compound K-17 at 0, 1, 5, 10, 50 and 100 µM (final DMSO 0.1%).
4. Incubate 48 h.
5. Add 10 µL MTT (5 mg/mL) per well and incubate 30 min at 37 °C.
6. Remove medium, add 100 µL DMSO, shake 10 min.
7. Read absorbance at 570 nm (reference 630 nm).

Controls: vehicle (0.1% DMSO), blank wells (medium only), positive control doxorubicin 1 µM.
