Materials and Methods

Procedure

1. Cell seeding: HeLa cells (ATCC CCL-2, passage 12) [F8: notebook_2024-05.txt:1; F13: notebook_2024-05.txt:1] were seeded at 5,000 cells per well in 96-well plates in 100 µL DMEM with 10% FBS [F17: protocol.md:3]; output: plates P1–P3 [F8: notebook_2024-05.txt:1; F13: notebook_2024-05.txt:1].

2. Attachment: plates were incubated for 24 h at 37 °C and 5% CO2 [F18: protocol.md:4; F26: protocol.md:4].

3. Treatment: cells were treated with compound K-17 (lot KX-0412) [F9: notebook_2024-05.txt:2; F14: notebook_2024-05.txt:2] at 0, 1, 5, 10, 50 and 100 µM with a final DMSO concentration of 0.1% [F19: protocol.md:5], then incubated for 48 h [F20: protocol.md:6; F27: protocol.md:6].

4. MTT labelling: 10 µL MTT (5 mg/mL) was added per well and incubated at 37 °C [protocol.md:7] for 45 min [F10: notebook_2024-05.txt:3; F15: notebook_2024-05.txt:3] [AUTHOR TO CONFIRM: C1 the SOP specifies 30 min; the notebook records 45 min for this run because the reader was booked. Text uses the notebook value].

5. Solubilisation: the medium was removed, 100 µL DMSO was added and the plates were shaken for 10 min [F22: protocol.md:8; F29: protocol.md:8] [AUTHOR TO CONFIRM: C2 the reader additionally shakes 5 s before reading (reader_settings.txt:8)].

6. Readout: absorbance was read at 570 nm with a 630 nm reference [F23: protocol.md:9] (decision rule for exclusion: wells with visible contamination were excluded; well H12 on plate P2 was excluded [F12: notebook_2024-05.txt:5]).

Instrument settings

Absorbance was measured on a BioTek Synergy H1 [F30: reader_settings.txt:1] with Gen5 3.11 [F31: reader_settings.txt:2; F32: reader_settings.txt:2] as an endpoint read [reader_settings.txt:3] at 570 nm [F33: reader_settings.txt:4; F34: reader_settings.txt:4; F35: reader_settings.txt:4] with a 630 nm reference wavelength [F36: reader_settings.txt:5; F37: reader_settings.txt:5; F38: reader_settings.txt:5], a read height of 8.5 mm [reader_settings.txt:6] and a chamber temperature of 25 °C [F41: reader_settings.txt:7; F42: reader_settings.txt:7; F43: reader_settings.txt:7].

Controls, replicates and statistics

Each plate included vehicle wells (0.1% DMSO), blank wells (medium only) and a doxorubicin 1 µM positive control [protocol.md:11]. Plates P1–P3 were three independent experiments [F11: notebook_2024-05.txt:4; F16: notebook_2024-05.txt:4]; viability is reported as mean ± SD with n = 3 per concentration [F48: viability_summary.csv:2; F49: viability_summary.csv:2; F50: viability_summary.csv:2]. Concentration–response curves were fitted with a four-parameter log-logistic model (LL.4) [analysis.R:5] to estimate the IC50 with delta-method intervals [analysis.R:6]. Treatment groups were compared with the vehicle by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's post hoc test [F6: analysis.R:7; F7: analysis.R:7] at alpha = 0.05 [F2: analysis.R:9]. Analyses used R 4.3.1 [F3: analysis.R:1] and drc 3.0-1 [F4: analysis.R:2] with set.seed(7) [F5: analysis.R:3].

Limitations

- Only one cell line (HeLa, passage 12) was tested [F8: notebook_2024-05.txt:1; F13: notebook_2024-05.txt:1]; responses of other cell types are unknown.

- The MTT incubation (45 min) deviated from the SOP (30 min) in all three experiments [F10: notebook_2024-05.txt:3; F15: notebook_2024-05.txt:3; F21: protocol.md:7; F28: protocol.md:7] [AUTHOR TO CONFIRM: C1 confirm the deviation applied to all plates], so absolute absorbance values may not be comparable with SOP runs.

- Each concentration rests on n = 3 independent experiments [F48: viability_summary.csv:2; F49: viability_summary.csv:2; F50: viability_summary.csv:2]; one well was lost to contamination [F12: notebook_2024-05.txt:5].
