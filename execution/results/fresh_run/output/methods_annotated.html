Methods

Procedure

1. Data split: the credit table credit_2019.csv [model.ipynb:4] was split into training and test sets with test_size = 0.25, stratified by the label [F12: model.ipynb:5; F13: model.ipynb:5; F14: model.ipynb:5], giving 22500 training and 7500 test rows [F8: model.ipynb:8].

2. Model selection: an XGBoost classifier was evaluated by stratified 5-fold cross-validation with shuffling [F15: model.ipynb:6; F16: model.ipynb:6; F17: model.ipynb:6] on the training set.

3. Final model and test: the classifier was refit with the parameters below [model.ipynb:11] and evaluated once on the test set by ROC AUC [F18: model.ipynb:12].
